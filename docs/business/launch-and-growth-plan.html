02The name

Verdict: rename before launch

“VEDEV” is already taken as a trademark (owner’s finding, 2026-11-04), and vedev.com and vedev.in are registered by others. Renaming now costs a domain and a settings change; later it costs rankings, links and readers. A similar earlier mark can also block your filing or lead to an infringement claim if its class covers publishing (41) or software (9, 42). Keep vedev.guru for a year and forward it to the new domain.

Local registrations in Maharashtra

Company / LLP Skip

Start as an individual (sole proprietor). Revisit when revenue, risk or hiring justify it.

Udyam (MSME) Optional

Free, but not needed: individuals already get the ₹4,500 trademark fee.

Shops & Establishments Likely not

A home-based solo blogger with no premises or staff is generally not covered. Ask your CA.

Profession Tax Ask CA

May apply to self-employed people above an income threshold (a small yearly amount).

GST At ₹20 lakh

AdSense income from Google Singapore is generally an export of services (zero-rated), but it counts toward ₹20 lakh. Verify with your CA.

PAN + bank Before ads

Needed for the AdSense tax form and for foreign payments (e-FIRA/FIRC).

06Ads: what AdSense and the code need

Before applying

  • 20 to 30 original, useful posts
  • Clear navigation; About, Contact and Privacy pages
  • Real (even small) traffic
  • No copied or mass-produced AI content

Consent: Europe, UK, Switzerland Official

Personalized ads there need a Google-certified consent tool (IAB TCF): required since 16 Jan 2024 in the EEA and UK, and since 31 Jul 2024 in Switzerland. Use AdSense's own "Privacy & messaging" option (certified, no extra cost; verify) and connect it to the existing banner.

09Several niche sites from one codebase

Don't copy the repository for each site: every fix would have to be made five times, and the copies would drift apart. Use one codebase and one Docker image, run as several separate sites.

Brand settings

"Vedev.Guru" is written directly in 22 places across 14 files in src/: footer, hero, wordmark, page titles and Open Graph, manifest, login and register, legal pages, RSS, mailer and emails. They move to runtime settings: SITE_NAME, SITE_SHORT_NAME, SITE_TAGLINE, SITE_PUBLISHER, SITE_THEME, SITE_CONTACT_EMAIL, ADSENSE_CLIENT_ID, plus logo and icons per site.

A theme per site

  • Design tokens: colors, fonts, radius and spacing as Tailwind 4 CSS variables.
  • Swappable layouts: header, home hero and grid, post card, post page and footer, picked by SITE_THEME.
  • Adding a site: one theme folder plus one .env, a domain, a Caddy route and a database. No platform changes.

Why not one process for every domain?

It's cheaper at large scale, but every query must be scoped by site, one bug can leak data between sites, and backups and data requests get harder. Separate deployments stay simple up to about 10 sites.

Rules for a network

  • Content: one niche per site, with original content; quickly made networks count as spam.
  • Timing: start site 2 once site 1 has traction (for example, growing search traffic and about 50 posts).
  • Links: cross-link only where it helps readers.
  • Releases: build once, test once, roll out to every site.

10Private posts

Built. The author ticks “Private: only I can see this post” in the editor.

Owner only Nobody else opens it

Readers, moderators and admins get “not found” for the post, its editor and revisions. It is in no list, search, sitemap, RSS or review queue, and never goes public (SHARED_POST, manageablePostFilter()).

Private images Served to the uploader alone

Sent with Cache-Control: private, no-store. Admins see such files in Uploads only as “Private”, without preview, details or download.

Note Still on a server

Copies saved while a post was public can’t be recalled: browsers, a CDN and the image optimizer’s cache can keep its images for up to a year. A breach or leaked backup exposes the database. Keep passwords, IDs and finances out of the blog.
